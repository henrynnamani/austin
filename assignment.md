**The Savings Tracker**

You earn ₦150,000 per month and spend ₦95,000.

1. Calculate how much you save per month
2. Calculate how much you'd save in a year
3. You want to buy a laptop that costs ₦380,000 — calculate how many months it will take to afford it (use integer division `//` and remainder `%`)
4. Print whether you can afford it in under 3 months (True or False)
5. Print whether your monthly savings is more than 30% of your income (True or False)
